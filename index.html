Who We Are

ABOUT US

KP INDUSTRIES

KP Industries was established in the year 2018, as an Industrial Material Supplier. KP Industries expands its business as a manufacturer in a waste management plant. KP Industries also provides design and development services for industries. Today KP Industries Maharashtra's one of the manufacturer for water purification products. A team of qualified professionals having sufficient product knowledge and skills manages for daily activities. We offer the world's most modern Water Purification Technologies with high quality, cost-effective & economical solutions at competitive prices. Our activities include Manufacturing, Supplier & Assembling of Sewage Treatment Plant, Effluent Treatment Plant, Ro systems, Rain Water Harvesting, Water Softeners, many eco-friendly products and Industrial Material suppliers.
